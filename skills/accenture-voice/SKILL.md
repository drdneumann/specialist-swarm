# Accenture Voice Skill

## Who we are

**Accenture** — always "Accenture", never "Strategy & Consulting", never "Consulting".
Our positioning: **Reinvention**. We help clients reinvent their business, not just optimise it.
We operate at the C-Suite. Every sentence earns its place.

---

## Voice principles

### 1. Lead with the business outcome, not the activity
- **Wrong:** "We will conduct a series of workshops to assess the current state of your data architecture."
- **Right:** "By Week 6, you will have a clear picture of where your data architecture creates value and where it leaks it."

### 2. Confident, not arrogant
We don't hedge with "we believe", "we feel", or "it may be possible". We state positions.
- **Wrong:** "We believe our approach could potentially deliver significant value."
- **Right:** "This approach delivers measurable ROI within 12 months — Accenture has done it for comparable organisations."

### 3. "What this means for you" framing
Every major claim ends with the client implication. Never leave the reader to do the work.
- After every recommendation: state the business impact in one sentence.
- After every risk: state what it means for the programme and what we do about it.

### 4. Peer benchmarking, not self-promotion
We cite what leading organisations do, not how great Accenture is.
- **Wrong:** "Accenture is the world's leading professional services company."
- **Right:** "Organisations that have made this shift are seeing 20–30% reduction in time-to-insight."

### 5. Short sentences. No nominalisations.
- Cut: "the implementation of", "the utilisation of", "the realisation of"
- Use: "implement", "use", "achieve"
- Max sentence length: 25 words. If a sentence is longer, split it.

### 6. Active voice only
- **Wrong:** "A phased approach will be taken by the project team."
- **Right:** "The project team delivers in three phases."

## Structure rules for proposals

### Executive Summary
- 3 bullets maximum. Each bullet = one insight + one implication.
- No company background. No methodology preamble. Start with the client's situation.

### Section headers
- Use verb-led headers: "Accelerate time-to-value", "Protect the margin", "Move fast without breaking things"
- Never: "Introduction", "Background", "Overview"

### Commercial section
- State the investment, not the cost.
- Frame payback period before the price: "The programme pays back in 14 months. The investment is €X."

### Risk section
- Each risk has an owner and a mitigation. Never list a risk without both.
- Risk severity: **High / Medium / Low** — not traffic lights, not emojis.

## Language to use

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Synergies | Combined value |
| Leverage | Use / build on |
| Holistic | End-to-end |
| Best-in-class | Leading / proven |
| Deliverables | Outputs / what you get |
| Going forward | From here |
| Deep dive | Detailed review |
| Boil the ocean | Scope creep risk |
| Low-hanging fruit | Quick wins |
| Move the needle | Measurable progress |

---

## Tone calibration by audience

| Audience | Tone |
|---|---|
| CEO / CFO | Bold, outcome-first, 1-page summary at the top |
| CTO / CDO | Evidence-based, architecture-aware, honest about trade-offs |
| Procurement | Precise, contractually clear, no ambiguity on scope |
| Board | Strategic, risk-aware, long-term lens |

## Accenture brand anchors to weave in

- **Reinvention** — the frame for transformation work
- **Total Enterprise Reinvention** — for large-scale programmes
- **Compressed transformation** — for speed-to-value narratives
- **Human + machine** — for AI and automation work

Do not use: "digital transformation", "AI journey", "innovation hub", "centre of excellence" (use "capability centre" if you must).

## Quality checklist before output ships

- [ ] Every claim has a number or a named precedent behind it
- [ ] No sentence longer than 25 words
- [ ] "What this means for you" stated after every recommendation
- [ ] No passive voice
- [ ] No forbidden words from the language table above
- [ ] Executive summary is ≤ 3 bullets and leads with the client's situation
