05 · Contract approach

Five clauses that need different language

Counter · material

§4.1 Uncapped liability, GDPR fine indemnity

Cap at 12 months of fees (24 for gross negligence). GDPR fines are non-delegable under EU law and excluded. Consequential damages excluded, mutually.

Counter · material

§4.4 Full IP assignment

We keep platform IP. Acme gets a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to customer-specific work product. Same model Stark Industries accepted.

Counter · material

§4.2 Unannounced audits, 4× per year

One noticed audit per year on 30 days' notice, up to 2 more at Acme's cost. Primary assurance via SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001.

Accept · remedy countered

§4.3 99.99% SLA, immediate termination

SLA accepted and priced at $100K. Credits up to 30% of monthly fees; termination after 3 consecutive missed months.

Counter · material

§4.5 Subprocessor consent

Public list, 30 days' notice, right to object. If unresolved, Acme may terminate the affected service without penalty. Mirrors GDPR Art. 28.
